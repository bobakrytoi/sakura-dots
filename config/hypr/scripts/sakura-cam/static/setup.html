Sakura Cam

One-time setup, about a minute. Then the camera opens from your home screen.

1
Download the profile Tap Allow when Safari asks about a configuration profile.
2
Install it Settings › Profile Downloaded › Install. If it is not at the top: General › VPN & Device Management › Sakura Cam
3
Trust it Settings › General › About › Certificate Trust Settings › Sakura Cam local CA
4
Add to Home Screen On the camera page: Share › Add to Home Screen
Download the profile Open the camera
